The 50/50 myth

Why isn't roulette 50/50?

Red and black feel like a coin flip. But the wheel has 37 pockets, not 36 — and the single green 0 belongs to neither colour.

Red and black are not 50%. Because the green 0 exists, each colour wins slightly less than half the time — 48.65%, not 50%. That missing 1.35% on each side is exactly where the casino's edge comes from.
Independence

The wheel has no memory

Every spin is a brand-new experiment. The ball doesn't know what happened before it.

After five reds in a row, red doesn't become less likely.

The next spin has exactly the same odds as the very first one:

Red
48.65%
Black
48.65%
0
2.70%

Believing "black is due" is the gambler's fallacy. Streaks happen by pure chance — and the odds reset every single spin.

Law of large numbers

The math

Expected value of a red bet

Bet 1 unit on red. You win +1 in 18 of 37 cases and lose 1 in the other 19 (black or 0).

E(X) = 18/37 × 1 + 19/37 × (−1)
E(X) = (18 − 19) / 37 = −1/37 ≈ −2.70%

In plain words: in the long run, the player's expected result is negative. On average you lose about 2.7 cents of every unit you bet. Short-term luck is real; the long-term trend is not in your favour.
